Our work has been accepted at NeurIPS 2026, motivating machine unlearning by design, where models can forget targeted data instantly through key deletion, without retraining or parameter updates. Looking forward to the discussions! 

And our recent work on machine unlearning leveraging distillation with held-out sets for forgetting has also been accepted at TMLR, take a look!

Checkout the preprints: [Models Designed to Forget: Machine Unlearning via Key Deletion](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.15033), [Reference-Guided Machine Unlearning](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.11210).
