I recently joined the organizing team of the [**ELLIS x UniReps Speaker Series**](https://unireps.org/speaker-series/), a monthly seminar bringing together researchers working on representation learning.

The series focuses on understanding **why neural networks develop similar internal representations**, and what this reveals about generalization, model alignment, transferability, and knowledge reuse across models. Each session features invited speakers presenting recent advances in the theory and empirical study of neural representations, followed by open discussion with the community.
