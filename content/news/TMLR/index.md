We demonstrate that structural supervision significantly enhances representation learning. By leveraging the inherent redundancy of multimodal and multiview data through joint masked reconstruction and cross-view alignment, we integrate auxiliary signals to improve performance. Checkout our preprint: [Structure is Supervision: Multiview Masked Autoencoders for Radiology](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.22294).

Additionally, we will be presenting our work on adapting generative models for accessibility in collaboration with UNICEF at CHI 2026, checkout the preprint: [Steering Generative Models for Accessibility: EasyRead Image
Generation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.13695).
